# Day 2 review notes (एयरपोर्ट पर प्रस्थान)

- `title_hi` written as "एयरपोर्ट पर प्रस्थान" to match Day 1's style; source title is "एयरपोर्ट पर बातचीत-2, प्रस्थान".
- Learner role id `passenger` (label यात्री, English "Passenger"), as in Day 8.
- Roles: कर्मचारी judged **m** (मैं देखता हूँ, जारी कर देता हूँ, व्यवस्था करता हूँ, बुलाता हूँ) → voice male_2. सुरक्षा अधिकारी and घोषणा are neutral (no self-referring gendered forms). The announcement is modelled as a role `announcer` (label "घोषणा").
- f variants (2): d02s1 "मैं मुंबई जा रही हूँ"; d02s5 security officer "अब आप आगे जा सकती हैं".
- No f added for "मेरा बोर्डिंग पास खो गया है" (गया agrees with पास) or "क्या मुझे खिड़की वाली सीट मिल सकती है?" (agrees with सीट).
- Hindi/English mismatches (English kept from source):
  - d02s1 "नमस्कार, प्रस्थान टर्मिनल कहाँ है?" = "Hello, …"; English has "Excuse me, …".
  - d02s4 "चार किलोग्राम।" = "Four kilograms."; English "You have four kilograms of excess baggage."
  - d02s7 "उड़ान लगभग एक घंटे विलंब से चलेगी।" = "will leave about an hour late"; English "Your flight has been delayed by about one hour."
  - d02s8 "आपका गेट संख्या 18 है।" = "Your gate is number 18"; English "Your flight will depart from Gate 18."
  - d02s5 "जी।" rendered "Certainly." in source.
- Source typo/spelling (not fixed): "वजन" without nukta (standard वज़न); Roman written "vajan" to match the Hindi as written. "टर्मिनल–2" uses an en dash.
- Numbers: Hindi keeps digits (टर्मिनल–2, 18, 12); Roman spells them out (do, athaarah, baarah). TTS may read the digits oddly; consider spelling them out in Hindi.
- Roman to check: Mumbaee (मुंबई), uplabdh (उपलब्ध), aavashyaktaa, karmchaaree, sheeghra, vastuen.
