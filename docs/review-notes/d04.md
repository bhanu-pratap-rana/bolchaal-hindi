# Day 4 review notes (बस अड्डे पर)

- Learner role `passenger` (यात्री). Other roles: `staff` (कर्मचारी; English column says "Clerk" and once "Staff") and `conductor` (परिचालक). Both are neutral: neither uses a gendered form about itself.
- Scene `title_hi` values drop the English in brackets from the source headings (e.g. "टिकट खरीदना (Buying a Ticket)" → "टिकट खरीदना"); "परिचालक (कंडक्टर) से बात करना" keeps its Hindi bracket.
- f variants (8): d04s1 staff "जाना चाहती हैं"; d04s5 "रख सकती हूँ"; d04s8 "बैठ सकती हूँ" and conductor "बैठ सकती हैं"; d04s9 "यात्रा कर सकती हूँ" and staff "कर सकती हैं"; d04s10 "भूल गई हूँ"; d04s11 staff "ले सकती हैं … रद्द करा सकती हैं".
- No f for "क्या मुझे खिड़की वाली सीट मिल सकती है?" (agrees with सीट).
- Hindi/English mismatches (English kept from source):
  - d04s12 conductor "आपकी यात्रा सुखद रही, धन्यवाद।" means "(I hope) your journey was pleasant, thank you." English says "You're welcome. Have a pleasant stay." Needs a teacher decision.
  - d04s1 "आप किस समय की बस से जाना चाहते हैं?" ("At what time…") vs "Which bus would you like to travel on?".
  - d04s4 "कृपया वही सीट दे दीजिए" ("Please give me that seat") vs "Please book that seat for me".
  - d04s8 / d04s9 the Hindi answers have no "जी हाँ" but the English starts with "Yes,".
  - d04s8 "कोई बात नहीं" ("No problem") vs "You're welcome".
- Numbers: Hindi keeps digits (10, ₹650, 9:30, 5, यूपी-32 एबी 4567); Roman spells them out. The bus number is Romanised as "UP battees AB chaar paanch chhah saat" — check that this is how it should be read aloud (many speakers say the digits in English).
- Roman to check: Dillee, Aagraa, Mathuraa, vaataanukoolit, adde/addaa (अड्डे/अड्डा), padaav (पड़ाव), voh (वह), vaidh (वैध).
