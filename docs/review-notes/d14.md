# Day 14 review notes: Asking about places to visit

- 5 scenes, 32 lines, 4 feminine variants. Learner role `traveller` (यात्री), other role `local` (स्थानीय व्यक्ति).
- The `local` role is set to grammar "neutral" because nothing in its lines shows gender.
- Source scene headings are not consistent: scenes 1–3 begin "स्थिति :" / "स्थितिः", scenes 4–5 have no prefix. The prefix was dropped from all `title_hi`.
- English for d14s2 line 4 and d14s5 line 6 was split over two rows in the source. The rows were joined.
- d14s2 line 5 "क्या वहाँ कोई गाइड मिल सकता है?" is impersonal in Hindi ("Is a guide available there?"). The English says "Can I find a guide there?". No `f` was added because the verb agrees with गाइड.
- d14s4 line 6: the English "it is quite crowded" only answers the question "is it open?" by implication. The pairing is fine; this is just for awareness.
- d14s5 line 1 "आराम कर सकूँ" is subjunctive and gender-neutral, so it has no `f`.
- Roman: रहता/रहती/रहते are written rahtaa/rahtee/rahte, following the spelling; पहले/पहली follow the earlier lessons (pehle/pehlee). किलोमीटर is kept as the English "kilometre"; किला is "kilaa" because the source has no nukta.
- Glossary items that do not appear in any line (no tap-to-gloss): स्थानीय स्थान, घूमने की जगह, दर्शनीय स्थल, दूरी, मिठाई (the lines have मिठाइयाँ), नदी का किनारा (the lines have नदी के किनारे), आराम करना.
