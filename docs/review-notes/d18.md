- 1 scene, 20 lines, 6 feminine variants. Learner role `tourist` (पर्यटक). Other roles: `local` (स्थानीय व्यक्ति), `staff` (कर्मचारी) and `hotel_staff` (होटल कर्मचारी).
- All three non-learner roles are "neutral" because none of them uses a gendered verb about itself.

- कर्मचारी and होटल कर्मचारी are kept as two roles because the source labels them differently: the cloakroom and waiting room suggest a station, the laundry a hotel. They could be one person.
- Scene title_hi is the day title, because the source has no scene heading.
